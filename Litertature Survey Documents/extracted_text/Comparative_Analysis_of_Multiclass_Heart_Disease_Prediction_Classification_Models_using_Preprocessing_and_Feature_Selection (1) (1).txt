Abstract— Prediction of heart disease and diagnosing it is a 
challenging task in the medical industry. All over the world, 
heart disease is considered as the deadliest disease in the life of 
the human being. Most deaths occur if the cause of the disease 
is not known in advance. The correct prediction of heart 
disease can save the life of the person. By selecting significant 
features and applying classification algorithms, heart disease 
could be predicted in an efficient way. In this paper, the 
preprocessing techniques like handling missing values, 
eliminating duplicate rows, and removing outliers have been 
applied to the UCI repository heart disease dataset. Machine 
learning classification algorithms such as SVM, Gradient 
Booster, Random forest, and Decision Tree were implemented 
in the dataset and the prediction accuracy was compared. The 
result shows that these models have produced more accuracy 
after preprocessing and feature selection techniques. 
Keywords— Heart disease, multiclass , preprocessing, feature 
selection, classification algorithms 
I. INTRODUCTION  
In personalised medicine, the analysis of healthcare data is 
becoming increasingly essential. Early detection of the disease 
is important. For example, in any disease, personalised 
treatment aims to give the proper treatment to the right patient 
by considering a variety of factors such as age, behaviour, and 
lifestyle. Modern technologies such as genetics, imaging and 
lifetime monitoring devices have generated enormous and 
complex amounts of health data in recent years, allowing 
researchers to access this data efficiently and make decisions 
that provide the best treatment for patients. 
Heart disease is widely treated as the most deadly disease 
in human history. During this type of disease, the heart cannot 
supply enough blood to the remaining organs of the human 
body to perform their normal functions [1]. On the basis of 
medical history, professional symptom analysis reports, and 
physical laboratory findings, invasive techniques are used to 
identify heart disease problems. However, at the moment of 
assessment, it is time-consuming, computationally complex 
and expensive [2]. In the medical industry predicting and 
diagnosing heart disease is the most difficult task and it is 
dependent on elements such as symptoms, physical 
examination and signs of the patient [3-5]. 
A wide range of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Deep Learning 
(DL) and Machine Learning (ML) approaches have been 
employed to efficiently evaluate the vast data in healthcare 
throughout the last decade. For example, in heart disease 
diagnosis, a logistic regression-based prediction model was 
used for automated early detection of heart disease. Medical 
imaging has also used machine learning techniques to allow 
automatic detection of object features. A variety of Deep 
learning algorithms have also been successfully used to 
forecast the disease at an early stage [6]. 
Predictive analytics is a type of technology that forecasts 
future unknowns based on historical data. For predicting the 
outcome of the existing data, it uses AI, ML and DL 
techniques. The main purpose of using these methods in health 
care is to enhance disease detection and classification, generate 
better predictions and helps personalised medicine, and the 
same is true for heart failure treatment [7, 8]. 
In this paper, the different Machine Learning classification 
methods were applied to the dataset. The performance is 
evaluated and the accuracy is compared, before and after, 
applying preprocessing and feature selection steps. 
II. HEART DISEASE DATASET 
The dataset consists of 920 patient details with 76 attributes. 
These are collected from Hungary, Cleveland, Switzerland and 
Long Beach datasets existing in the UCI repository. However, 
along with the target attribute, only a subset of 14 attributes, 
namely age and sex of the person, resting blood pressure, type 
of chest pain, level of cholesterol, and blood sugar in fasting 
stage, etc. are used. Table 1 lists all 14 attributes with their 
description. 
The embedded sensors in an ECG (Electrocardiogram), by 
placing it on a patient's chest, help in detecting irregular 
heartbeat and stroke [9]. Angina is a discomfort caused by a 
lack of oxygen in the blood supply to the heart muscle. 
Atypical, typical, asymptomatic, and non-angina pain are the 
different forms of angina. Heart disease is more likely in 
people who have high blood pressure and fast heart rate. The 
risk of coronary heart disease is directly proportional to heart 
rate. Symptoms of heart disease are usually chest tightness and 
it extends from the abdomen to the shoulders.

B. Preprocessing 
Preprocessing techniques are useful for processing the raw 
data and make it suitable for a machine learning model [10]. 
Data preprocessing methods have been devised and employed, 
to improve the efficiency of classifier models for heart disease 
prediction. Data Cleaning is one of the preprocessing 
techniques, in which it deals with missing data, finding and 
removing outliers, identifying and eliminating noise, and 
resolving data inconsistencies. 
 
1) Handling missing values: Because missing data can lead to 
errors in statistical calculation and misleading visualization, 
checking the integrity and consistency of data is necessary 
before processing it. To check whether the dataset has null 
values, different methods are available. The df.isna( ).sum( ) is 
used to see how many null values are there for the attributes in 
a dataset. The Missingno library can also be used to visualize 
missing data. The horizontal lines shown in Fig. 2 represent 
the values that are missing. This library is useful for viewing 
missing values and determining correlations between the 
missing values in various columns. 
 
 
Fig. 2. Columns with missing data using Missingno library 
 
To handle missing data, any one of the following 
techniques can be used:  (i) missing data elimination (2) 
toleration of missing data and (3) missing data imputation. In 
the elimination technique, the records that have more missing 
value attributes are deleted, but in the toleration technique, it is 
possible to manage data with all missing values. Imputation is 
the process of substituting missing data with a statistical 
estimation of those missing data in a data set without loss of 
any information. There are two categories of imputation

2)  Identifying and removing Outliers:  An outlier is a data 
point that stands out from the rest of the data. There are two 
major forms of outlier analysis- univariate (single variable) and 
multivariate (two or more variables). Outliers can be found 
using either graphical tools or mathematical functions. We 
have a boxplot method or a scatter plot method in visualisation 
tools. A scatter plot is a graph that represents values for two 
variables in a collection of data using Cartesian Coordinates. 
An outlier is depicted as a point in a box plot, but other 
populations are aggregated and displayed as boxes. Fig. 4 
shows the outliers in the continuous features such as age, 
trestbps, chol, thalach  and oldpeak.

A Z-score or an InterQuartile Range (IQR) score can be 
used in mathematical functions. The Z-score can be used to 
find data distributions with a mean of 0 and standard deviation 
of 1. The difference between upper and lower quartiles is 
measured by IQR, a statistical dispersion measure. When 
applying the IQR method, due to data inconsistency, certain

V. FEATURE SELECTION 
The purpose of feature selection is always to find the most 
important heart disease features. Moreover, feature selection 
helps in the construction of a more accurate model by 
removing less relevant information, reducing training time and 
improving learning performance [11]. Pearson's Correlation 
coefficient is one of the popular feature selection techniques 
which can be used to measure the association between 
continuous variables. The Correlation method is applied to 657 
samples and 13 distinct features. The more correlated 10 
features such as age, sex, cp, restecg, thalach, exang, oldpeak, 
slope, thal, and num were selected to predict heart disease. 
VI. RESULTS AND COMPARISON 
The Machine Learning Supervised Algorithms such as 
Random Forest, Support Vector Machine (SVM), Decision 
Tree and Gradient Booster applied to the dataset. A decision 
tree is a supervised learning technique commonly used to solve 
classification problems. It can be used with categorical or 
continuous dependent variables. SVM is a supervised learning 
approach, its main goal is to minimize generalization errors 
while performing regression and classification tasks. SVM has 
shown great performance in the medical field in recent years 
[12, 13] for disease prediction. Gradient boosting classifiers is 
a collection of machine learning algorithms that build a strong 
predictor by combining many weak predictors. Random Forest 
is a machine learning approach that combines numerous 
classifiers to tackle a complex problem and improve the 
performance of the model.

In the prediction of cardiac disease, machine learning 
algorithms play a vital role. In this paper, preprocessing and 
feature selection techniques are applied to ensure data 
consistency. Different Machine learning algorithms were tried

after preprocessing was compared. The result shows that the 
prediction models have produced more accuracy after 
implementing preprocessing and feature selection techniques.

also be tried to improve accuracy.
